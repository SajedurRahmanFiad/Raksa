How we work

Recruitment & selection

A disciplined process designed to deliver the right workforce.

We manage every manpower assignment through information, screening, interview, verification and training before final deployment.

01

Information

Collect exact job description, candidate count, salary, start date and other conditions from the employer.

02

Advertisement

Publish vacancy notices through newspapers, regional media, branch networks and approved channels.

03

Pre-selection

Screen applicants at the advertised place and time, and identify candidates for final employer review.

04

Registration

Candidates submit applications, CVs and required documents for review and database processing.

05

Final interview

Qualified candidates attend a final interview with the employer representative where required.

06

Medical & verification

Selected personnel proceed to medical checks, background investigation and documentation checks.

07

Training

Workers are trained in specific skills, confidence, and language readiness before deployment.

08

Deployment

Final candidates are placed with clear documentation, employer confirmation and site-ready preparation.
