Construction profile

Technical manpower and site execution for demanding scopes.

RAKSA is positioned to undertake electrical, mechanical, construction and maintenance related activities while supplying skilled and unskilled personnel for building, infrastructure, industrial and project support requirements.

The objective is simple: provide the right kind of person for the right kind of job, complete work on time and maintain strong client communication from first requirement to final deployment.

Manpower profile

Flexible staffing designed around employer requirements.

The manpower model starts with the employer's exact requirement, then moves through sourcing, screening, interview, medical, verification, documentation, training and deployment.

Candidate pipelines can include qualified workers from Bangladesh, India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Nepal and regional sources depending on client needs and compliance requirements.
